# Morrow – Privacy Policy

**Developer:** Dhruv Akbari  
**App Name:** Morrow  
**Last Updated:** November 10, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Morrow, a project and task management application designed to help users organize projects and manage their daily tasks.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Morrow handles information when you use the application.

## 2. Information Collection

Morrow does not require users to create an account or log in.

The application does not collect, request, or transmit personal information to the developer's servers.

Projects, tasks, and related information created by users are stored locally on their devices.

## 3. Local Data Storage

All project and task information is stored locally on your device.

Morrow does not use cloud storage or synchronize your information with external servers.

Your locally stored information is used solely to provide the application's project and task management functionality.

## 4. Data Sharing

Morrow does not sell, rent, or share your project or task information with third parties.

The application does not transmit locally stored project or task information to the developer.

## 5. Advertisements

Morrow does not display advertisements or use advertising services.

## 6. Data Security and Retention

Your project and task information remains in your device's local storage.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

You can manage or delete your projects and tasks using the application's available features. Clearing the application's storage or uninstalling it may permanently delete locally stored information.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Morrow is a general-purpose productivity application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
